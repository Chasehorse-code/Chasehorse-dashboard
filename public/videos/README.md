# ChaseHorse — Hero Background Video Specifications

This directory holds optional ambient hero background video loops for clients with high-speed connections and devices that do not have `prefers-reduced-motion` enabled.

---

## 1. Technical Requirements
- **Resolution:** 1080p (1920 × 1080 px) or 720p (1280 × 720 px)
- **Duration:** 12 to 15 seconds seamless loop (crossfaded ends)
- **File Size:** Under 5.0 MB per video (heavily optimized H.264 / MP4 or VP9 / WebM)
- **Frame Rate:** 24 fps or 30 fps
- **Audio:** None (muted track / strip audio stream completely)
- **Visual Treatment:** Neutral grade, high contrast, warm tint conforming to `--charcoal` (`#171717`) and `--ivory` (`#F4F1EA`).
