 3. No Clear Explanation of the Three-Tier Service Structure 
 One of the most important aspects of our business model is that our services are organized into three 
 different tiers. However, the current website does not clearly explain this structure. 
 The website should include a dedicated section that introduces and explains: 
 ●  Tier 1 – Core Business Services 
 ●  Tier 2 – Strategic & Growth Services 
 ●  Tier 3 – Advanced Operations & Technology Services 
 The user should immediately understand: 
 ●  Why the services are divided into three tiers. 
 ●  What type of services are included in each tier. 
 ●  Who each tier is designed for. 
 ●  How the three tiers are connected. 
 ●  How a business can select the appropriate service or combination of services. 
 This explanation should be presented visually using a premium layout, clear hierarchy, animations, 
 icons, cards, or an interactive section. 
 4. No Individual Page and Workﬂow for Each Service 
 Currently, the website does not provide a dedicated page for every individual service. This is a major 
 requirement. 
 Each service should have its own detailed page containing: 
 ●  Service title and overview. 
 ●  Detailed explanation of the service. 
 ●  Problems the service solves. 
 ●  Who the service is designed for. 
 ●  Key features and deliverables. 
 ●  Benefits to the client. 
 ●  Step-by-step workflow. 
 ●  Expected outcomes. 
 ●  Related services. 
 ●  Call-to-action or booking option. 
 The workflow is especially important. Users should be able to clearly understand how the service 
 works from the initial consultation to final delivery. 
 The exact workflow should be customized according to each individual service rather than using the 
 same generic process everywhere. 
 Home → Three-Tier Overview → Tier Page → Individual Service Page-> Individual subservice 
 Page→ Workflow / Booking / Enquiry 
 This hierarchy is necessary to make the website easier to understand and navigate. 
 5. Merchandise Page Does Not Look or Function Like an E-Commerce Website 
 The current merchandise page does not provide a proper e-commerce experience. 
 The merchandise section should look and function like a professional online store. It should include: 
 ●  Proper product cards. 
 ●  Search and filtering options. 
 ●  Product sorting. 
 ●  Individual product detail pages. 
 ●  Product variants such as size or colour where applicable. 
 ●  Checkout process. 
 ●  Payment integration. 
 ●  Order confirmation. 
 ●  Order tracking where applicable. 
 The page should visually feel like a real e-commerce store rather than a basic product showcase or 
 catalogue.
